How It Works

A guided path from idea to structured next steps.

BranchOps is not positioned as instant business formation or automatic approval. It is a guided readiness system with human review gates where the risk is higher.

Step 1

Submit your business or idea

Start with the founder, business status, business type, assets, revenue, risk, proof, and goals.

Step 2

BranchOps classifies it

The system identifies whether the request is a service business, IP asset, training lane, licensing opportunity, or another operating category.

Step 3

Get a readiness score

The score is a practical progress signal, not a legal certification or promise of funding, approval, or business success.

Step 4

Complete proof-based steps

Move toward launch, funding readiness, licensing, training, internal verification, or public review only after the right evidence exists.

Verification

Proof review, not surveillance.

BranchOps verification is not surveillance. It is a structured proof process that helps businesses document important milestones, work completion, and readiness.

  • Self-submitted proof starts the file. Notes, documents, photos, links, screenshots, receipts, and contracts can support the operating record when appropriate.
  • Higher-risk claims need stronger review. Some items may require customer confirmation, admin review, advisor review, or third-party evidence before status advances.
  • Public certificates are not issued at signup. Intake starts the process. Public verification requires review, evidence, approval, and renewal rules that are not automatic.

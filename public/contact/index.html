Before You Submit

The best inquiries answer three questions up front.

This makes routing faster and improves the odds that the request reaches the right next step without extra clarification.

Question 1

What lane is involved?

Name the company, asset class, licensing category, or operating system the request relates to.

Question 2

What is the commercial objective?

State whether the request is partnership, rights use, media, distribution, systems implementation, or something else.

Question 3

What are the constraints?

Include timing, geography, channel, scale, or any other detail that affects fit and review priority.

Not every inquiry will progress into a deeper discussion. Branch Off Holdings reviews opportunities selectively based on fit, clarity, rights alignment, and execution seriousness.

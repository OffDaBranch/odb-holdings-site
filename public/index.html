Qualified Inquiry Path

How serious conversations should enter the system.

The strongest inquiries already know the lane involved, the commercial use case, the timeline, and the outcome they want from the conversation.

Step 1

Identify the lane

Start with the company, asset class, or licensing category involved before moving into contact.

Step 2

Define the use case

State what you want to do, where it applies, and whether the request is partnership, licensing, media, or operations-related.

Step 3

Submit a qualified inquiry

Use the intake path with enough detail to evaluate fit, scope, and commercial seriousness.

Step 4

Move into structured discussion

Qualified opportunities can then progress into scoped conversations, deeper materials, and commercial terms.

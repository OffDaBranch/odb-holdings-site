Privacy

How inquiry data is handled.

This notice explains how Branch Off Holdings handles information submitted through the contact and inquiry flows on this website.

What is covered Name, email, company details, inquiry type, message content, and related contact routing data that you provide directly.
What it supports Response, qualification, partnership review, licensing review, and controlled workflow routing.

What we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, inquiry category, and any message or opportunity details you submit directly through the website.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, review partnership and licensing opportunities, route requests into controlled workflows, and improve the inquiry process.

Data handling

Submitted inquiries may be stored inside BranchOps-controlled systems and may also be mirrored to operational tools used for follow-up, task routing, and workflow management.

Questions

If you have questions about this notice or your submitted information, contact admin@branchoffholdings.com.
